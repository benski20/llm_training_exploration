How private inference works, with encrypted masks

The model's matrix multiplies run on our GPUs. Your prompts, answers and every intermediate value stay readable only inside your network. Two flows make that work: the online path for each token, and the mask supply that feeds it.

Your network (Private Gateway) Our cloud (Masked Compute Service) Secret that never leaves your network

1. The online path: one masked round trip per layer

For every token, each of the model's linear layers (4 per transformer block) is sent to our GPU as a one-time-masked vector. The gateway removes the mask from the reply and checks it before using it.

2. The mask supply: our GPU computes W·r without seeing r

Each mask needs its product W·r. Computing that locally costs as much as running the model, which is what we are outsourcing. So the gateway encrypts r with module-LWE, our GPU multiplies the ciphertext, and only the gateway can decrypt the result.
